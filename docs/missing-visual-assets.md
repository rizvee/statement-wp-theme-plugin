## 2. Studio Photography & Generation Prompts

### Asset 01: Monogram Jacquard Jacket — Side Profile & Motion
- **Subject:** Male model in standing three-quarter side profile, wearing heavy black and charcoal-grey monogram jacquard zip jacket with relaxed black trousers.
- **Lighting:** Soft daylight studio, neutral warm grey/ivory background `#FAF9F6`, subtle shadow falloff.
- **Camera Settings:** 85mm portrait lens, f/4.0, crisp fabric texture focus, natural skin tones.
- **Prompt:**
  ```text
  Editorial studio fashion photograph, side three-quarter profile of a tall male model wearing a luxury structured jacquard jacket with repeating dark charcoal geometric monogram weave, minimalist unbranded black tailored trousers, clean minimal gallery backdrop in gallery ivory and warm white, soft directional studio lighting, tactile wool fabric weave, luxury minimalist aesthetic in the style of Lemaire and Fear of God Athletics, 8k resolution, Hasselblad medium format --ar 4:5 --stylize 150
  ```

### Asset 02: Panelled Hood Jacket — Rear Architecture
- **Subject:** Model seen from behind wearing an ivory/white heavy French terry hooded jacket with dark geometric patterned sleeve panels, hood raised.
- **Lighting:** Sculptural side lighting highlighting seam construction and hood drape against clean limestone wall.
- **Prompt:**
  ```text
  Minimalist luxury fashion photography, rear view of model wearing a structured heavy white French terry zip hoodie with contrasting dark geometric jacquard sleeves, hood up, standing before minimalist limestone architecture, soft architectural shadows, clean warm ivory daylight, tactile premium cotton loopback and jacquard texture, high-end lookbook aesthetic, 8k --ar 4:5
  ```

### Asset 03: Luxury Packaging & Unboxing Suite
- **Subject:** Heavy rigid presentation box in gallery ivory with blind debossed geometric insignia, open to reveal tissue paper seal, canvas dust bag, and stitched leather provenance certificate.
- **Lighting:** Top-down flat lay with soft diffused natural morning light.
- **Prompt:**
  ```text
  Top-down product photograph of a luxury fashion unboxing suite, rigid warm white presentation box with subtle blind debossed geometric logo, unbleached heavy canvas garment bag, cream leather label with brass stitching, minimalist stone table surface, tactile craftsmanship, ultra-clean high-end packaging design --ar 4:5 --stylize 120
  ```
